## User design and engineering preferences

Approach future changes as the lead developer and lead UI/UX designer: use maintainable, clearly structured code, intentional visual hierarchy, consistent interactions, accessibility, responsive layouts, and proportionate validation. Preserve the site's warm, cosy, academic paper-and-ink character; the Beyond page takes its atmosphere from Oxford in the 1920s. Motion should serve the subject, respect reduced-motion preferences, and avoid distracting from reading.
